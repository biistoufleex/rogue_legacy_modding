[list]

[*][b]Shotgun[/b] — 5 pellets in a fan, short range (15 MP)
[*][b]SMG[/b] — full auto while you hold the spell button (3 MP per bullet)
[*]Debug key: [b]F9[/b] cycles the current spell between the 3 guns
[*]Own save slot: your Steam saves (and Steam Cloud) are never modified
[/list]
Want to play with a friend? See the companion mod [b]Rogue Legacy - Co-op[/b].

[list=1]

[*]Launch [b]RogueLegacy.exe[/b]. Optional: Steam > [i]Add a Non-Steam Game[/i].
[/list]
Windows 64-bit only. Steam achievements are disabled in the mod.
If Windows shows [i]"Windows protected your PC"[/i] (the exe is not signed): [b]More info > Run anyway[/b].

[*]Guns are only offered to the Knight and the Mage (and their upgraded classes).
