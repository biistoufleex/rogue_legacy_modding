## Résumé court (Brief overview, 350 caractères max)

> Unofficial mod: 3 Neon Abyss-style guns (Pistol, Shotgun, SMG) and drop-in local 2-player co-op (plug a second
> gamepad, press START). Built from Cellar Door Games' official Rogue Legacy 1 source code. No game files
> included — you need to own Rogue Legacy.

This mod is based on the Rogue Legacy 1 source code released by Cellar Door Games ([url=https://github.com/flibitijibibo/RogueLegacy1]github.com/flibitijibibo/RogueLegacy1[/url]) under the Rogue Legacy Source Code License (non-commercial). It is free, contains [b]no game files[/b] (graphics, music, sounds), and you must [b]own Rogue Legacy[/b] to play it.

[size=3][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]3 guns[/b] usable as spells by the Knight and the Mage:
[list]
[*][b]Pistol[/b] — fast straight bullet (5 MP)
[*][b]Shotgun[/b] — 5 pellets, short range (15 MP)
[*][b]SMG[/b] — full auto while you hold the spell button (3 MP per bullet)
[/list]
[*][b]Local 2-player co-op[/b] (drop-in, arcade style):
[list]
[*]Plug a 2nd gamepad and press [b]START[/b] — Player 2 appears next to Player 1, anywhere.
[*]One gamepad only? Player 1 on keyboard, press [b]SELECT + START[/b] on the pad.
[*]In the starting camp, a short press on [b]SELECT[/b] cycles Player 2's class (Knight / Mage / Barbarian). Upgraded class powers (Paladin, Archmage, Barbarian King) if unlocked in your manor.
[*]Shared Metal Slug-style camera with invisible screen walls, enemies target the nearest player, Player 2 gets knocked out and comes back after 8 s.
[*]Hold [b]SELECT[/b] 3 s to leave.
[/list]
[*][b]Play online[/b] with [url=https://parsec.app/]Parsec[/url]: the remote player's gamepad becomes gamepad 2.
[*]Separate save slot: your Steam saves (and Steam Cloud) are never modified.
[*]English and French texts.
[/list]

[size=3][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract the archive anywhere ([b]not[/b] inside your Steam game folder).
[*]Right-click [b]install.ps1[/b] > [i]Run with PowerShell[/i]. It finds your Steam copy of Rogue Legacy, copies its [i]Content[/i] folder next to the mod and creates the mod's own save slot (a copy of your saves).
[*]Launch [b]RogueLegacy.exe[/b]. Optional: Steam > [i]Add a Non-Steam Game[/i] > RogueLegacy.exe.
[/list]
Windows 64-bit only. Steam achievements are disabled in the mod.
